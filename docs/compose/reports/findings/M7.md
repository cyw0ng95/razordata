# F7: What is the practical feasibility of implementing PGM-index or ALEX learned indexes within Razordata's page-cache storage model (4KB pages, sync.Pool buffers, no CGo)? Given that PGM's O(log log N) lookup is memory-bound by error-bounded search on internal model parameters, does the advantage hold at embedded-workload cardinalities (10K-10M keys) with Razordata's single-goroutine write path?

## Findings

### [1] PGM-index lookup is provably O(log log N) but memory-bound in practice
- quote: "querying PGM-Indexes is highly memory-bound, where the internal error-bounded search operations often become the bottleneck"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00846
- source_type: primary (peer-reviewed research, October 2024)
- published: 2024-10-01
- confidence: high

### [2] PGM++ achieves 2.31x speedup over original PGM by optimizing search strategies
- quote: "PGM++ establishes a new Pareto frontier. At comparable space costs, PGM++ speeds up index lookup queries by up to 2.31x and 1.56x when compared to the original PGM-Index and state-of-the-art learned indexes"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00846
- source_type: primary (peer-reviewed research)
- published: 2024-10-01
- confidence: high

### [3] PGM-index achieves O(log log N) lookup with O(N) space for N sorted keys
- quote: "for a set of N sorted keys, the PGM-Index can, with high probability, achieve a lookup time of O(log log N) while using O(N) space"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00846
- source_type: primary (peer-reviewed research)
- published: 2024-10-01
- confidence: high

### [4] ALEX beats B+Tree by up to 4.1X on read-write workloads with up to 2000X smaller index size
- quote: "Across the spectrum of read-write workloads, ALEX beats B+Trees by up to 4.1X while never performing worse, with up to 2000X smaller index size"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08898
- source_type: primary (SIGMOD 2020, Microsoft Research)
- published: 2019-05-21 (revised 2020-05-21)
- confidence: high

### [5] Learned indexes on disk underperform B+-tree in most cases
- quote: "directly applying the existing learned indexes on disk suffers from several drawbacks and cannot outperform a standard B+-tree in most cases"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.01237
- source_type: primary (peer-reviewed research, 2023)
- published: 2023-05-02
- confidence: high

### [6] PGM achieves best performance in write-only workloads on disk
- quote: "PGM achieves the best performance in write-only workloads while LIPP significantly outperforms others in lookup-only workloads"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.01237
- source_type: primary (peer-reviewed research)
- published: 2023-05-02
- confidence: high

### [7] PGM-index improves FITing-tree space by 63.3% and B-tree by more than four orders of magnitude
- quote: "the PGM-index improves the space of the FITing-tree by 63.3% and of the B-tree by more than four orders of magnitude"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.06169
- source_type: primary (PVLDB 2020, original PGM paper)
- published: 2019-10-14
- confidence: high

### [8] ALEX is vulnerable to worst-case adversarial insertions causing 1641X performance degradation
- quote: "the time ACA generates pathological insertions to increase the disparity between the actual key distribution and the linear models of data nodes, deteriorating the runtime performance by up to 1,641X compared to ALEX operating under legitimate workloads"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12433
- source_type: primary (VLDB 2024)
- published: 2024-03-19
- confidence: high

### [9] Cache-aware I/O modeling improves PGM throughput by 1.17x over naive tuning
- quote: "CAM-guided tuning improves PGM throughput by 1.17x over multicriteria PGM tuning"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21924
- source_type: primary (peer-reviewed research, June 2026)
- published: 2026-06-20
- confidence: high

### [10] AULID (on-disk learned index) outperforms PGM by up to 8.63x on disk workloads
- quote: "AULID is up to 2.11x, 8.63x, 1.72x, 5.51x, and 8.02x more efficient than FITing-tree, PGM, B+-tree, ALEX, and LIPP"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.02604
- source_type: primary (peer-reviewed research, 2023)
- published: 2023-06-05
- confidence: high

### [11] PGM-index is a header-only C++ library with 873 GitHub stars and multiple variants
- quote: "State-of-the-art learned data structure that enables fast lookup, predecessor, range searches and updates in arrays of billions of items using orders of magnitude less space than traditional indexes"
- url: https://github.com/gvinciguerra/PGM-index
- source_type: primary (official implementation)
- published: unknown (active repository)
- confidence: high

### [12] At embedded scales (10K-10M keys), O(log log N) advantage over B-tree diminishes significantly
- quote: (derived from theoretical analysis) log log 10K ≈ 3.7 vs log128(10K) ≈ 2.3 for B-tree with fanout 128; log log 10M ≈ 4.5 vs log128(10M) ≈ 3.3
- url: (theoretical calculation based on findings [3] and [7])
- source_type: derived
- published: unknown
- confidence: medium
